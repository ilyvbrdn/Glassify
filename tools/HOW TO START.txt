Glassify - Spotify on Windows, your way: glass in your colours, a mini player or a bar, out of the way when you game

1. Keep this whole folder somewhere you can write to (Documents, Downloads, your user folder).
   Not Program Files: Glassify saves your login and settings in a "data" folder next to Glassify.exe.
2. Double-click Glassify.exe and sign in to Spotify (Premium needed).
3. First launch only: if Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" -> "Run anyway".
   (The app isn't code-signed yet.) After that, Glassify updates itself.

Tips
- Gear button (top right): Look, Player, Gaming and More tabs.
- Ctrl+Alt+M show/hide the player, Ctrl+Alt+B mini player <-> bar, Ctrl+Alt+G click-through ghost
  mini player, Ctrl+Alt+L like, Ctrl+Alt+Up/Down volume. Media keys work too.
- To keep it on your taskbar: Start -> right-click Glassify -> Pin to taskbar.
- Never share the "data" folder: it holds your Spotify login.

Needs Windows 10 or 11 (with the Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime, built into Windows 11).
